Carolina Club Pickleball is a student organization at the University of South Carolina that provides opportunities for students to play pickleball, a fast-growing sport that combines elements of tennis, badminton, and ping pong. The club welcomes players of all skill levels, from beginners to advanced players, and offers regular practice sessions, social events, and competitive tournaments. Members can improve their skills, meet new friends, and enjoy the benefits of an active lifestyle.

The club is committed to promoting a positive and inclusive environment for all members. It encourages sportsmanship, teamwork, and respect for others, both on and off the court. Members are expected to follow the club's code of conduct and adhere to the rules of the game. The club also collaborates with other student organizations and community groups to promote pickleball and raise awareness about the benefits of physical activity and healthy living.
